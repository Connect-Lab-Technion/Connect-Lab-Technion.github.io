Large swarms cannot rely on every robot receiving a global command. Instead, a small number of informed agents must influence the group through the same local interactions that maintain cohesion and avoid collisions.

## The question

How much authority does one leader need to steer a swarm, and how do graph structure, sensing range, and agent dynamics affect that influence?

## What the project can include

- Compare leader-selection strategies on large interaction graphs.
- Study the trade-off between cohesion and responsiveness to a command.
- Design scalable simulations for heterogeneous or switching swarms.
- Test selected controllers on the lab's mobile-robot platforms.
