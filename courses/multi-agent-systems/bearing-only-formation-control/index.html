About the examples and measurements

The nonlinear controller uses only current bearings and fixed desired bearings in a common frame. Four agents interact on the complete undirected graph K4 with gain one. All three initial configurations share the target centroid and RMS scale. The target is obtained by centering the four positions (-1.5, -0.8), (1.2, -0.9), (1.4, 0.9), and (-1.1, 1.2).

The two moving examples use the original saved adaptive-integration trajectories. No positions are recentered or rescaled during integration. The third example displays the exact stationary reflected solution rather than allowing floating-point perturbations to move an unstable equilibrium. Colored trails show past motion, dashed edges show the target, and all examples use the same fixed world scale.

The right panel tracks the stacked bearing and position error norms, their base-10 logarithms, and numerical drift in the centroid and RMS scale. For the exact reflection, the bearing error remains 2 sqrt(6), even though the control is zero.
