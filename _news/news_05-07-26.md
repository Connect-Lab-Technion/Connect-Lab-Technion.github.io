The Connect Lab gathered for lunch at Fairuz Restaurant and Cafe in Daliyat al-Karmel, a wonderful Druze restaurant. It was a good chance to spend time together outside the lab and mark several moments for the group.

![Connect Lab members at Fairuz Restaurant and Cafe in Daliyat al-Karmel](https://live.staticflickr.com/65535/55375569588_66c5a696e1_c.jpg)

During the lunch, we celebrated Zamir Martinez's successful PhD candidacy exam, welcomed Eva Bochner to the lab as a summer research program intern for the month, and wished Daniel well as he begins his sabbatical year.
