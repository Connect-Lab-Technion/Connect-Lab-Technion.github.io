This week I am visiting the School of Mathematical Sciences at Lancaster University, where I will give a talk in the Pure Mathematics Seminar series. My seminar, *Formation Control through Group Symmetries*, presents a new geometric framework for distributed formation control that replaces traditional metric constraints with symmetry constraints derived from finite point groups. More information about the seminar is available on the [Lancaster seminar webpage](https://www.lancaster.ac.uk/maths/about-us/events/pure-mathematics-seminar-dan-zelazo/).
