This week I am visiting the School of Mathematical Sciences at Lancaster University, where I will give a talk in the Pure Mathematics Seminar series.

The seminar, *Formation Control through Group Symmetries*, presents a geometric framework for distributed formation control in which desired formations are specified through symmetry constraints derived from finite point groups, rather than through traditional metric constraints such as distances, bearings, or angles.

More information is available on the [Lancaster University seminar page](https://www.lancaster.ac.uk/maths/about-us/events/pure-mathematics-seminar-dan-zelazo/).
