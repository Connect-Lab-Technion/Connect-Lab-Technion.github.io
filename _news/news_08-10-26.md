On October 13 I will give a seminar at the Institute for Systems Theory and Automatic Control at the University of Stuttgart. My talk, *Toward a Geometric Theory of Passivity*, presents an extension of classical passivity in which dissipation is measured relative to geometric objects such as synchronization manifolds, formation manifolds, and limit cycles, rather than isolated points.

I always love going back to Stuttgart to visit. I spent part of my postdoctoral years at the institute, and it is a special pleasure to return, reconnect with friends and colleagues, and share our latest work.

More information is available on the [University of Stuttgart seminar page](https://www.ist.uni-stuttgart.de/events/Talk-of-Prof.-Daniel-Zelazo-00001/).
