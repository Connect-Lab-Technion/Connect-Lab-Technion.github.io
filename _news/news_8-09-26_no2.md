I am delighted to be an invited speaker at the workshop [*Rigidity Theory: Structures, Data, and Control*](https://sites.google.com/view/rigidityworkshop2026/home), taking place at Lancaster University from September 14–18, 2026.

The workshop brings together researchers working in rigidity theory and related areas, including applications in data, engineering, and formation control, for a week of talks and discussions.

I always love interacting with the rigidity theory community. Its distinctive combination of geometry, combinatorics, and applications has deeply influenced the way I think about formation control and networked systems. I am looking forward to sharing ideas, learning about recent developments, and—hopefully—beginning some new collaborations during the visit.

Further details, including the program and practical information, are available on the [workshop website](https://sites.google.com/view/rigidityworkshop2026/home).
